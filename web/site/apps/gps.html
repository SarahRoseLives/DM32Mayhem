What it does

Shows the GPS fix, a sky plot of the satellites, and a Maidenhead locator, and streams raw NMEA ($GPRMC and $GPGGA) out of the USB serial at 115200 8N1 while the screen is open.

How to use it

  1. Open RX → GPS.
  2. Press Up / Down to page between Status, Sky and Grid.
  3. Point a serial program at the radio's port to read the NMEA feed.

Screen fields

Status
Sats used/seen, latitude, longitude, altitude, speed, HDOP and UTC.
Sky
A sky plot with GPS and BeiDou counts.
Grid
The Maidenhead locator, e.g. EN91.
Serial
NMEA 115200 8N1 while the screen is open.

NMEA is emitted only while the GPS screen is open; with no fix the status is V and the fields are zero.
