Apps

What's in the radio

DM32Mayhem puts a set of small, single-purpose apps in one tile grid. Pick one, set its rows, and it keys, tunes and unkeys the radio for you. Here is each one, what it does and how to use it.

Two-way apps: the ones where the radio keys on your command.

TRX → FM

FM transceiver

A real FM transceiver with live PTT, repeater offset and CTCSS.

How to use it →

TRX → BBS

Packet BBS

How to use it →

KISS TNC

A KISS packet TNC over USB for APRS programs on a computer or phone.

One-shot transmitters. Each queues a job and the radio keys, tunes and unkeys itself.

Experimental (EXP)

The EXP menu holds experimental apps — ACARS, POCSAG and SSTV today. They are in the tree and they may change, or not decode yet. They are not documented here on purpose.
