KISS TNC

A KISS packet TNC over USB for APRS programs on a computer or phone.

← All apps Flash your radio →

What it does

Turns the radio into a KISS packet TNC on the USB serial port, so APRS software can send and receive through it. The screen shows the channel, callsign and packet counters.

How to use it

  1. Open TRX → KISS TNC.
  2. Point your APRS program at the radio's serial port in KISS mode.
  3. Watch Heard, KISS in and KISS out to confirm traffic.

Screen fields

Status
The TNC state.
Frequency / Callsign
The APRS channel and station address.
Heard
Frames heard on the channel.
KISS in / out
Frames from and to the computer.
Serial
The USB serial setting: KISS 115200 8N1.

Needs a build with KISS and hostlink; the callsign is the APRS callsign.
