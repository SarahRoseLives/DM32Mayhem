What it does

A two-tone FSK RTTY app. Transmit ITA2 text at 45.45 or 50 baud with a settable mark and shift, or listen and decode RTTY from the FM discriminator.

On receive the text scrolls in a log and opens full screen from the Messages row.

How to use it

  1. Transmit: open TX → RTTY, set Frequency, Message, Baud, Mark, Shift and Repeat, then select Transmit RTTY.
  2. Receive: open RX → RTTY, set Frequency, Baud, Mark, Shift and Invert; it starts listening.
  3. Select Stop listening to stop, or Messages to read the log full screen.

Screen fields

Frequency
The channel.
Message
The ITA2 text to send.
Baud
45.45 or 50 baud.
Mark / Shift
The tone and its shift, e.g. 2125 / 170 Hz.
Repeat
Times to send the message.
Invert
Flip the tones on receive.
Messages / Status
The log, and the sync/error counts.
