TV

An analog TV test-pattern carrier on cable and UHF channels.

← All apps Flash your radio →

What it does

Transmits a TV test pattern as an AM video carrier on a cable or UHF channel, with a selectable pattern and bit timing. Useful for testing old TVs and video gear.

How to use it

  1. Open TX → TV.
  2. Pick a Channel; the Frequency follows.
  3. Choose a Pattern and set the Bit timing.
  4. Select Send pattern to start, and again to stop.

Screen fields

Channel
Cable 54–66 and UHF 14/15.
Frequency
Derived from the channel.
Pattern
Bars, Stripes, Checker, Split or Field.
Bit
Video bit timing in microseconds.
Send pattern
Start or stop the carrier.

Check your licence before transmitting; this keys a video carrier.
